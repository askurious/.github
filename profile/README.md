**Askurious** is a web platform for social science research: plan a study, build the questionnaire, take it to the field, check the data quality and analyse the results, all in one place.
It is developed by the [Chair of Communication Science, RWTH Aachen University](https://www.comm.rwth-aachen.de), for research and teaching.

## Repositories

| Repository | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [askurious](https://github.com/askurious/askurious) | The platform: questionnaire editor, fieldwork, live votes, interviews, analysis and export. |
| [askurious-diary-android](https://github.com/askurious/askurious-diary-android) | The Android app for diary studies (experience sampling): prompts at fixed or random times, offline answers. |

## Try it

- Platform: [askurious.de](https://askurious.de)
- All question types in action: [ask.askurious.de/s/introduction](https://ask.askurious.de/s/introduction)

## What it covers

- **Plan:** study plan with hypotheses, power analysis, validated scales, pre-registration snapshot.
- **Build:** more than forty element types, flow logic, quotas, twelve survey languages, accessibility after BITV 2.0 / WCAG 2.1 AA.
- **Field:** links and QR codes, panel providers, data-quality checks, live votes, interviews, diary studies, paper questionnaires.
- **Analyse and share:** results in APA style, choice models, method section, export to SPSS, jamovi and R, open data for Zenodo and Coscine.
- **Data protection:** deletion codes, retention periods, and a privacy concept built from the code.
